# Cricut Design Space — How It Works & Feature Guide

> Cricut Design Space is the free companion software used to design projects and send
> them to a Cricut cutting machine. This guide covers how it works and what you get on
> the **free** tier versus the paid **Cricut Access** subscription (what most people mean
> by "Pro").
>
> _Note: Cricut changes features, library counts, and pricing over time. Treat specific
> numbers here as approximate and confirm current details at cricut.com._

---

## 1. What Is Design Space?

Design Space is Cricut's design-and-cut app. You lay out a project on a digital
**Canvas**, then the software slices it into cut/draw/score instructions and sends them
over USB or Bluetooth to your Cricut machine, which physically cuts, writes, or scores the
material on a mat.

**Available on:**
- Windows and macOS (desktop app)
- iOS and Android (mobile/tablet app)
- A Chromebook/Chrome OS version exists with some limitations
- The core app itself is **free** to download and use — you only pay for a subscription
  or à la carte content if you want premium assets.

**Works with Cricut machines such as:**
- Maker series (Maker, Maker 3) — widest material + tool support
- Explore series (Explore 3, Air 2, etc.)
- Joy / Joy Xtra — compact machines
- Cricut mug press, EasyPress and heat products integrate with project workflows

---

## 2. How It Works (Typical Workflow)

1. **Start a project** — open a blank Canvas or pick a pre-made project/template.
2. **Add content** — text, shapes, images from the library, or your own uploaded files.
3. **Design & edit** — size, rotate, align, layer, and combine elements using the edit
   tools (see below).
4. **Choose materials & tools** — assign each layer a line type: Cut, Draw (pen), Score,
   Foil, Engrave, etc., depending on your machine's capabilities.
5. **Make It** — Design Space lays out each color/material onto separate virtual mats.
6. **Set material + load mat** — tell the machine what you're cutting (vinyl, cardstock,
   etc.), load the mat and tool, and press go.
7. **Weed / assemble** — remove excess material and assemble the finished project.

## 3. Core Design Tools (Available to Everyone)

These editing tools are part of the free app regardless of subscription:

| Tool | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Slice** | Cut one shape out of another (splits into pieces). |
| **Weld** | Merge multiple shapes/letters into one continuous cut. |
| **Attach** | Hold layers in position relative to each other (and keep draw/score aligned). |
| **Group / Ungroup** | Organize layers so they move/resize together. |
| **Flatten** | Turn a multi-layer design into a single printable image (for Print Then Cut). |
| **Contour** | Hide/remove unwanted cut lines or interior cutouts of a shape. |
| **Align & Distribute** | Line up and evenly space objects. |
| **Offset** | Create an outline/shadow layer around text or shapes. |
| **Color Sync** | Consolidate layers to the fewest colors/mats. |
| **Text tools** | Fonts, letter/line spacing, curve text, bold/italic where supported. |
| **Layers panel** | Manage stacking, visibility, and line types per layer. |

**Line / operation types** (hardware-dependent): Cut, Draw (pen), Score, Engrave,
Deboss, Wavy cut, Perforate, Foil, and **Print Then Cut** (print a design on your home
printer, then let the machine cut around it).

## 4. Free Version — What You Get

The free tier is fully functional for making real projects. It includes:

- ✅ **Full Canvas and all core editing tools** (slice, weld, attach, etc.)
- ✅ **Upload your own images** — SVG, PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP, DXF
- ✅ **Upload and use your own fonts** (system fonts installed on your device)
- ✅ **A rotating set of free images, fonts, and ready-to-make projects**
- ✅ **Print Then Cut**, **Snapmat**, and material presets
- ✅ **Buy premium content à la carte** — pay per individual image (~$0.99 each is common),
  font, or project without a subscription
- ✅ Save, sync, and open projects across devices with a free Cricut ID

**Limitations of free:**
- Most of Cricut's large library (premium images, fonts, projects) is **locked** unless
  you buy items individually or subscribe.
- No membership discounts on content or Cricut.com purchases.
- No bulk library access — costs add up if you frequently use premium designs.

## 5. Cricut Access (The Paid / "Pro" Tier)

**Cricut Access** is the subscription that unlocks Cricut's content library and perks.
It does **not** change what your machine can physically do — it's about content,
convenience, and discounts. Common plan structure:

### Cricut Access — Standard
- 🔓 Access to **1,000,000+ images**, **thousands of fonts**, and a large set of
  **ready-to-make projects** included in your membership
- 💸 **Member discounts** — typically ~10% off licensed/premium content and often a
  discount on Cricut.com product and material purchases
- 🚚 Shipping perks (e.g., free shipping over a threshold) on Cricut.com
- 🎯 Priority member care / faster support
- Billed **monthly or annually** (annual is cheaper per month)

### Cricut Access Premium (higher tier)
Includes everything in Standard, plus deeper savings, such as:
- 📉 Larger discounts on licensed images, fonts, and ready-to-make content
- 🛒 Discounts (often up to ~50%) on select Cricut products and materials
- 🚚 Free economy shipping on Cricut.com orders

> Plan names, percentages, and library counts shift over time and can vary by region.
> Always check cricut.com/access for the current offering before subscribing.

## 7. Who Should Choose What?

- **Stick with Free if:** you mostly upload your own designs/SVGs and fonts, make
  occasional projects, or only need premium content now and then (buy à la carte).
- **Get Cricut Access if:** you design frequently, want unlimited use of Cricut's
  library without per-item fees, and value the content discounts and shipping perks.
  A single busy month of buying premium images can cost more than a subscription.

---

## 8. Quick Tips

- You can **mix free and premium**: subscribers still upload their own files, and
  free users can still buy single items.
- **Uploaded SVGs are always free to cut** — a great way to use designs from other
  sources (respecting their licenses).
- Premium items used inside a saved project **stop cutting if your subscription lapses**,
  unless you purchased them outright.
- Design Space needs an internet connection for most features; limited **offline** use
  is supported in the mobile app after syncing.
